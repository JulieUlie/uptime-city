    Uptime City

    Build homes, power and Wi-Fi lampposts. Mine raw materials, craft gadgets in a Maker Workshop, wire up IT integrations, and let automation rules run the city for you.

    • Click a building in the left list, then click a green lot.

    • The weather on the board is the real weather in your city right now.
